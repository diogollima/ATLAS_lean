──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 PULLBACK REGIME
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ENTRY REQUIRES ALL conditions to be TRUE:

 1. PRICE STRUCTURE (1H Timeframe)
    ├─ close > ema200                                  [price above 200-bar EMA]
    ├─ close ∈ [ema50 × 0.975 to ema50 × 1.025]       [price within 2.5% of 50-bar EMA]
    ├─ rsi ≥ 28 AND rsi ≤ 55                           [RSI in pullback mode]
    └─ bb_pctb < 0.40                                  [price closer to lower Bollinger Band]

 2. MACRO CONTEXT (4H Timeframe)
    ├─ close_4h > ema50_4h                            [4H price above 50-bar EMA]
    └─ ema50_4h > ema200_4h                           [4H 50-EMA > 200-EMA, confirms uptrend]

 3. REGIME CONFIRMATION (1H & 4H)
    ├─ adx14 < 25                                     [not in strong directional move]
    └─ chop14 > 55                                    [market consolidating/choppy]

 4. DAILY MACRO FILTER (D Timeframe) — blocks most entries
    ├─ daily_close > daily_ema21                      [daily price above 21-bar EMA]
    └─ daily_ema21 > daily_ema50                      [21-EMA > 50-EMA, macro bullish]

 ✓ PULLBACK ENTRY FIRES when:
   All price structure + all macro + all regime + all daily = TRUE

 Typical Pattern: ██████ (support) ▼ (pullback) ↓ ▼ (dip buy) ↑ (recovery)


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 TRENDING REGIME
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ENTRY REQUIRES ALL conditions to be TRUE:

 1. PRICE STRUCTURE (1H Timeframe)
    ├─ close > ema50                                  [price above 50-bar EMA]
    ├─ close > ema200                                 [price above 200-bar EMA]
    ├─ rsi ≥ 50 AND rsi ≤ 75                          [RSI in momentum mode]
    └─ atr14 ≥ atr_sma20                              [volatility expanding, not contracting]

 2. REGIME CONFIRMATION (4H Timeframe)
    ├─ adx14_4h > 25                                  [strong directional move]
    └─ chop14_4h < 38.2                               [strongly trending, not choppy]

 3. DAILY MACRO FILTER (D Timeframe)
    ├─ daily_close > daily_ema21                      [daily price above 21-bar EMA]
    └─ daily_ema21 > daily_ema50                      [21-EMA > 50-EMA, macro bullish]

 ✓ TRENDING ENTRY FIRES when:
   All price structure + all regime + all daily = TRUE

 Typical Pattern: ██████ (uptrend) ↗ (momentum) → ↗ (continue up)


──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 RANGING REGIME
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DEFAULT state when PULLBACK and TRENDING conditions are NOT met.

Examples:
 • Downtrends (daily EMA21 < EMA50)
 • Sideways consolidation without pullback setup
 • Choppy markets (CHOP > 61.8)
 • Weak trends (ADX < 20)
 • Oversold (RSI < 28) or overbought (RSI > 75) extremes

 ❌ NO ENTRIES in RANGING regime (backtest shows low win rate)


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  INDICATOR THRESHOLDS QUICK REFERENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MOVING AVERAGES
├─ EMA50 (1H):     Support/resistance for pullbacks
├─ EMA200 (1H):    Long-term uptrend confirmation
├─ EMA50 (4H):     Macro uptrend baseline
├─ EMA200 (4H):    Macro trend direction
└─ EMA21 (Daily):  Macro filter; blocks entries when broken

MOMENTUM (RSI)
├─ RSI < 28:       Oversold (avoid, risk of continued selling)
├─ 28-55:          PULLBACK safe zone (dip buying)
├─ 50-75:          TRENDING zone (momentum riding)
└─ RSI > 75:       Overbought (exhaustion risk)

VOLATILITY
├─ ATR(14):        Absolute volatility magnitude
├─ ATR SMA(20):    Volatility baseline
├─ ATR ≥ SMA:      Expanding volatility (good for TRENDING)
└─ ATR < SMA:      Contracting volatility (avoid)

TREND STRENGTH
├─ ADX < 20:       Weak or no trend (RANGING)
├─ ADX 20-25:      Transition zone
├─ ADX > 25:       Strong directional move (TRENDING)
└─ ADX > 40:       Very strong (late-stage exhaustion risk)

CHOPPINESS (Fibonacci-based consolidation)
├─ CHOP < 38.2:    Strongly trending (NOT choppy)
├─ 38.2-61.8:      Transition / mixed
└─ CHOP > 61.8:    Consolidating / choppy (PULLBACK safe)

BOLLINGER BANDS
├─ BB %B < 0.40:   Near lower band (cheap, good for pullback entries)
├─ 0.40-0.60:      Middle band (neutral)
└─ BB %B > 0.60:   Near upper band (extended, exhaustion risk)

RE-ENTRY COOLDOWN:
